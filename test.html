01 About

A builder across hardware and software.

I'm a EXTC Engineering student who likes the seams — the places where vision, robotics, electronics and the web meet and have to work as one.

Most people treat those fields as separate. My favourite projects are the ones that refuse to: a camera that reads a hand gesture, a bot that decides where to go, a circuit that authenticates a vote. I learn by building things end to end — from the silicon to the interface.

That has meant training a KNN classifier to recognise gestures in real time, simulating a battalion of autonomous bots in ROS2, and wiring up secure authentication on an ESP32. My goal is to grow into an engineer who builds intelligent, dependable systems — and to keep working at that edge where robotics, AI and thoughtful software design overlap.

Financial fraud is a major challenge in digital payment systems. This project uses Machine Learning to classify transactions as fraudulent or legitimate using the PaySim Fraud Detection Dataset. The solution includes data preprocessing, feature engineering, model training, and deployment through an interactive Streamlit web application for real-time predictions.

Machine Learning · Digital Twin

AI-powered Predictive Maintenance Digital Twin

Unexpected equipment failures can lead to significant downtime, safety risks, and maintenance costs. This project develops an AI-powered Digital Twin capable of predicting the Remaining Useful Life (RUL) of aircraft engines using historical sensor telemetry from the NASA C-MAPSS FD001 dataset. The project covers the complete predictive maintenance pipeline—from data engineering and machine learning to explainable AI and an interactive digital twin dashboard for real-time visualization and decision support.

Python Streamlit Pandas NumPy XGBoost Scikit-learn SHAP Plotly Matplotlib ReportLab
